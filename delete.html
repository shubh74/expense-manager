Delete Your Data

Paisook by Aadhya Ventures · Effective 14 November 2026 · Version legal-2026-11c

How to delete your Paisook account and data, what is erased, and how long it takes.

Contents

  2. 2. Delete without the app
  3. 3. Delete only some data

1. Delete everything from the app

Open Paisook → Settings → Withdraw consent & delete account → Delete. This happens immediately and:

  • erases your account record, consent record, points and backup key from our servers;
  • removes you from any family ledger or group and deletes what you shared there (if you own it, the whole family ledger is deleted);
  • deletes your sign-in account;
  • erases the ledger, receipts, reminders, budgets and settings on this phone, and disconnects Gmail.

2. Delete without the app

If you no longer have the app or your phone, email the support email shown in Settings → Help & feedback from the email address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my account". We may ask you to confirm it is you. We acknowledge within 48 hours and complete the deletion within 30 days, then confirm by email.

3. Delete only some data

  • One entry: swipe it left in the ledger, or open it and tap Delete.
  • Everything on this phone: Settings → Erase all data on this phone.
  • Gmail access: Settings → Bank emails → Disconnect, or myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Your Drive backup: Settings → Backup → Delete backup from my Drive, or in Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → Paisook → Delete hidden app data.
  • Android phone backup: turn it off in your phone's Settings → Google → Backup.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all data stored on the phone.

4. What we keep, and for how long

Only what the law makes us keep: records of Pro payments you made (UPI reference, amount and date) for as long as tax and accounting law requires, generally up to 8 years, and security logs for one year. These are kept securely, used for nothing else, and then erased. Copies in our providers' backups are overwritten on their normal cycle.

5. Before you delete

Deletion cannot be undone. If you want to keep your records, use Settings → Download my data first to save a copy as Excel or JSON.
