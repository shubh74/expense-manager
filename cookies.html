1. The short version

Paisook does not use cookies to track you. The app has no advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and our website sets no cookies at all.

2. What cookies and similar technologies are

Cookies are small files a website saves in your browser. Similar technologies include local storage, pixels, device identifiers and software development kits (SDKs) that collect data about how you use an app.

3. Our website

  • Cookies: none, first-party or third-party.
  • Local storage: one entry that remembers you closed the cookie notice, so it does not appear on every visit. It contains no personal data, never leaves your browser, and is strictly necessary for the notice to work. You can clear it in your browser at any time.
  • Third-party resources: none. Our fonts and images are served from our own site, so visiting does not send your data to Google Fonts, social networks or ad networks.
  • Hosting logs: GitHub Pages and Vercel, which host the site, automatically log technical request data (such as IP address and browser) to deliver and protect it. This is not a cookie; see section 9 of the Privacy Policy.

4. The app

  • No cookies, advertising IDs, analytics, attribution, heat-map or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • The app keeps its settings and your ledger in its own private storage on your phone, which is needed for it to work, and its keys in Android's secure keystore.
  • Google Sign-In and Firebase use their own secure tokens to keep you signed in; they are not used for tracking or advertising.

5. Your choices and consent

Because we only use what is strictly necessary, no consent is needed under India's DPDP Act, the EU ePrivacy Directive or the GDPR. We still show a notice so you know. If we ever add a cookie or tool that is not strictly necessary, such as analytics, it will stay off until you choose "Accept", and "Reject" will be just as easy and prominent. You can change your choice at any time.
