About Paisook

Paisook by Aadhya Ventures · Effective 14 November 2026 · Version legal-2026-11c

The story behind the name, the people who build it, and what we promise you.

The name

Paisook (पैसुक) brings together two words: "paisa" (money) and "book", the khata or bahi that Indian families have kept for generations to note every rupee in and out. Say it aloud and you also hear "sukh": peace of mind. That is the whole idea: one honest money book for your home, that gives you calm instead of stress.

The logo

The mark is a bold "P" for Paisook. Inside it, three rising bars stand for money that grows when you can see where it goes, and the soft fold at its base is a page turning in your money book. It sits on a deep peacock teal, calm and trustworthy, the way a family ledger should feel.

Why we built it

Most of our spending today happens through UPI, cards and bank transfers, spread across many apps and many SMS. Families end up guessing where the month went. We wanted one place where payments record themselves from your bank's own alerts, cash is just as easy to note, and your family can see the shared picture, without selling your data or pushing loans at you.

What we promise

  • Your ledger stays on your phone. Messages are read on the device and never uploaded.
  • Your backup goes to your own Google Drive, encrypted on your phone, free for everyone.
  • We never sell your data, never show ads, and never push loans or credit.
  • Clear consent under India's DPDP Act, 2023. You can see, download or delete your data any time.
  • Made for India: UPI, major Indian banks, and English plus six Indian languages.
