Install in a minute

Four quick steps.

Paisook is shared directly while our Play Store listing is on the way, so Android asks a couple of extra questions the first time.

Open the fileTap the download notification, or find Paisook.apk in Files → Downloads.
Allow this sourceIf asked, tap Settings → turn on "Allow from this source", then go back.
Play Protect?Tap "More details" → "Install anyway". This happens for any app outside the Play Store.
Open & agreeRead and accept the Terms and Privacy Policy, then sign in with Google or email.
Download stuck at 100%?

Chrome checks every app file before finishing and waits for you to confirm — that's the pause.

  1. Look for "Download anyway" at the bottom of the screen and tap it.
  2. Don't see it? Chrome ⋮ → Downloads → tap the file → Download anyway / Keep.
  3. Still stuck? Delete it, switch to Wi-Fi, and download again in Chrome (not inside WhatsApp).
Turn on automatic SMS tracking

Android blocks SMS access for apps from outside the Play Store until you allow it once. It takes 20 seconds.

  1. Long-press the Paisook icon → App info.
  2. Tap ⋮ (top-right) → "Allow restricted settings" and confirm.
  3. Permissions → SMS → Allow. Allow Notifications too.
  4. Open Paisook — this month's alerts come in on their own.

Rather not give SMS access? Long-press any bank SMS → Share → Paisook.

Is my data safe?

Your ledger and messages stay on your phone. We only keep your name, email and plan to run your account. Read the Privacy Policy.
