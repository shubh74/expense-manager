How it works

Pay as usual. Paisook writes it down.

No typing, no bank passwords, no linking accounts. Just the alerts your bank already sends you.

1 · You pay UPI, card, net banking, or cash noted in two taps.
2 · Your bank sends an alert By SMS, email or app notification, read only on your phone. Rs.320 debited from A/c XX1234 to Swiggy · UPI Ref 4312…
3 · It's in your money book Payee, category and account filled in. Counted once, even if it arrives twice.
Paid for a friend?

Only your share is your spending.

Every other app counts the whole bill as your expense, and their payback as your income. Paisook doesn't.

  • Mark what was theirs: all of it, half, or an exact amount.
  • Their payback is matched on its own when it arrives by UPI, or Paisook asks you.
  • Paid back in cash? One tap adds it to cash in hand, not to income.

Private by design

Your money is your business.

We built Paisook so we never need to see your finances to make it work.

Is my data safe?

Your ledger and messages stay on your phone. We only keep your name, email and plan to run your account. Read the Privacy Policy.

Is Paisook free?

Yes! Tracking, budgets, splits, the family ledger and more are free. An optional Pro plan adds extras like card bills, credit health and the tax helper, and new members can try Pro free, with no payment details needed.

Which banks and apps work?

All major Indian banks and cards that send SMS or email alerts (HDFC, SBI, ICICI, Axis, Kotak, IndusInd, Jio Payments Bank and more), plus Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm and other UPI apps.

Can my family use it together?

Yes. Create a private family ledger, choose what each person shares, and settle shared costs by UPI in one tap. Trip groups work with friends too.
