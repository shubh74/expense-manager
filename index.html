Download stuck at 100%?

⏳Chrome checks every app file before finishing, and waits for you to confirm. That's the pause at 100%.

  1. Look for “Download anyway”Chrome shows “File might be harmful” at the bottom of the screen — tap Download anyway.
  2. Don't see it? Open Chrome's downloadsChrome ⋮ (top-right) → Downloads → tap the file → Download anyway / Keep.
  3. Still stuck?Delete it from Downloads, switch to Wi-Fi, and download again in Chrome (not inside WhatsApp).

Install in a minute

  1. Open the downloaded fileTap the download notification, or find ExpenseManager.apk in Files → Downloads.
  2. Allow installs from your browserIf asked, tap Settings → turn on “Allow from this source”, then go back.
  3. If Play Protect warns youTap “More details” → “Install anyway”. This happens for any app not from the Play Store.
  4. Open the app and sign inTick the consent box, then use Google or email.

Turn on automatic tracking

⚠️Android blocks SMS access for apps installed outside the Play Store until you allow it once. It takes 20 seconds.

  1. Long-press the Expense Manager icon → App infoOr: Settings → Apps → Expense Manager.
  2. Tap ⋮ (top-right) → “Allow restricted settings”Confirm with your PIN or fingerprint.
  3. Permissions → SMS → AllowAlso allow Notifications to get a note for each payment.
  4. Open the app — donePast alerts from this month come in, and every new one is recorded on its own.

💡Don’t want to give SMS access? In Messages, long-press any bank SMS → Share → Expense Manager. It’s recorded instantly.
